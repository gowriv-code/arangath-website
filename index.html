01
Civil · Hydraulic Infrastructure Malappuram, Kerala

Chamravattom Bridge — Kerala

One of Kerala's longest bridges at nearly a kilometre, built across the Bharathapuzha river in southern India. It carries road traffic while controlling the flow of water through 70 sluice gates — a dual-purpose design that cuts the driving distance between two of Kerala's major cities by 38km and irrigates thousands of hectares of farmland. A complex civil and hydraulic engineering contract requiring precise coordination between road, bridge and water management systems.

978m Length Road + Irrigation Civil · Hydraulic Kerala Government
02
Water Supply · Rural Infrastructure Kollam, Kerala

Rural Water Supply — Kollam, Kerala

Drinking water infrastructure delivered under India's Jal Jeevan Mission — a government programme bringing piped clean water to every rural household across the country. Works in Kollam district included laying underground water mains, building pumping stations and elevated storage tanks, drilling tube wells, and connecting thousands of homes to clean water for the first time. A community-scale civil engineering contract with direct impact on public health.

Rural Water Supply Pipeline Networks Pumping Stations Government of India
03
Water Treatment · Process Engineering Kollam, Kerala

100 MLD Water Treatment Plant — Kollam, Kerala

A large-scale drinking water treatment facility built to serve Kollam city and surrounding communities in southern Kerala — processing 100 million litres of raw water every day into safe drinking water. The plant takes water from source, aerates it to remove odours, passes it through three large circular settling tanks, filters it through 24 filter beds, and disinfects it before pumping it through a new trunk main to the city. Delivered under India's AMRUT programme, addressing chronic water scarcity across one of Kerala's largest urban areas.

100 MLD Capacity Drinking Water AMRUT Programme Kollam Corporation
